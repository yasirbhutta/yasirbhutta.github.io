## 1. Group size and slide count

- Work in groups of **3–4 students**.
- Prepare **at least 20 PowerPoint slides**. Aim for **24 slides** to make it easier to divide the work among group members.

## 2. Presentation content and design

- Teach **one main idea per slide**.
- Use readable text and explain code in your own words.
- Use visuals—such as flowcharts, diagrams, screenshots, or annotated code—to make ideas easier to understand.
- Vary your slide layouts. Keep animations simple and use them only when they help explain a point.
- Cite sources for any facts, images, or code you did not create.

## 3. Sharing the work

- Every student must help prepare the slides and speak during the presentation.
- Divide the slides so each student has a similar amount of work and speaking time.
- Use the 24-slide plan to help share the presentation evenly.
- Rehearse as a group and practice smooth handoffs between speakers.

## 4. In-class presentation and submission

- Present your PowerPoint slides in class.
- Record the presentation with voice narration.  
- Submit the narrated recording through Google Classroom by **16 October 2026**.

## Final checklist

Before submitting, confirm that:

- The group has **3–4 students**.
- The presentation has **at least 20 PowerPoint slides**.
- Every student contributed and speaks during the in-class presentation.
- Sources are cited where needed.
- The narrated recording is submitted to the assigned Teams assignment.
