# Google Meet Assignments

## Topic 1: Create, Join, and Share Your Screen in Google Meet

### Tasks

1. **Install Google Meet:** Install the Google Meet app on your mobile phone.
2. **Create a Meeting:** Create a new meeting and copy the meeting link.
3. **Share the Link:** Send the meeting link to a class fellow.
4. **Join the Meeting:** Join the meeting with your class fellow.
5. **Share Your Screen:** Share your mobile screen during the meeting.
6. **Record Proof:** Record a **10-second video** showing the meeting and screen-sharing activity.

### Submission Requirements
- **Proof of Completion:** Keep the 10-second video recording as evidence of completing the tasks.
- **Deadline:** Complete all tasks before the next lecture.
